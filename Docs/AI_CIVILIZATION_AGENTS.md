# AI Civilization Agents

AI Civilization Agents protect long-term knowledge memory.

## Agent Roles
Archive Agent, Historian Agent, Knowledge Curator, Migration Agent, Integrity Agent, Preservation Agent, Education Agent, Governance Agent, Constitution Agent, and Future Compatibility Agent.

## Boundaries
Agents assist with preservation, migration, integrity, education, standards, and governance. They cannot erase provenance, override governance, or publish constitutional changes without human review.

## Evaluation
Agents are benchmarked for preservation quality, evidence handling, lineage fidelity, historical stability, accessibility, migration safety, and governance transparency.
