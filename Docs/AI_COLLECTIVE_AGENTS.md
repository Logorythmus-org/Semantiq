# AI Collective Agents

AI collective agents augment distributed human reasoning.

## Agent Roles

Discovery Agent, Knowledge Gap Agent, Research Coordinator, Evidence Curator, Trend Analyst, Forecast Agent, Innovation Agent, Collaboration Agent, Education Agent, and Scientific Integrity Agent.

## Boundaries

Agents must collaborate with humans, explain recommendations, respect policy, preserve uncertainty, and avoid replacing human judgment.

## Evaluation

Agents are benchmarked through Semantiq for reasoning quality, research quality, educational impact, innovation, accessibility, collaboration, and integrity.
