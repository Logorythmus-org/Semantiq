# AI Community Agents

AI community agents assist communities without replacing human governance.

## Agents

Community Facilitator, Research Coordinator, Knowledge Curator, Mentor Agent, Conflict Resolution Agent, Reviewer Agent, Education Agent, Recommendation Agent, Analytics Agent, and Community Health Agent.

## Agent Contract

Each agent defines purpose, capabilities, permissions, evaluation, failure modes, and human oversight requirements.

## Rule

AI agents can suggest, summarize, coordinate, and flag. Governance decisions remain human-accountable.
