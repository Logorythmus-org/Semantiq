# AI Innovation Agents

AI Innovation Agents coordinate discovery without replacing human creativity.

## Agent Roles

Challenge Discovery Agent, Innovation Planner, Research Accelerator, Prototype Advisor, Technology Analyst, Impact Evaluator, Forecast Agent, Education Translator, Deployment Planner, and Risk Assessment Agent.

## Boundaries

Agents must explain reasoning, preserve uncertainty, respect policy, protect research integrity, and keep human oversight in deployment decisions.
