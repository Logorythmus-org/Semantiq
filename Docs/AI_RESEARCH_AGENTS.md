# AI Research Agents

AI research agents accelerate work while preserving human oversight.

## Agent Roles
Research Planner Agent, Evidence Agent, Literature Agent, Dataset Agent, Hypothesis Agent, Experiment Agent, Review Agent, Statistics Agent, Writing Agent, Publication Agent, Visualization Agent, and Recommendation Agent.

## Agent Contract
Each agent defines purpose, capabilities, inputs, outputs, permissions, failure modes, and evaluation metrics.

## Safety
No hidden AI modifications. Publications, reviews, experiment conclusions, and research claims require human approval according to policy.
