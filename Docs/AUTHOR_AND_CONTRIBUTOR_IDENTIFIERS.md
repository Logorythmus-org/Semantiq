# Author & Contributor Identifiers Policy

This document defines the author, contributor, and ORCID identification policy for **SemantIQ Benchmarks**.

---

## Contributor Roles & CRediT Taxonomy

SemantIQ adopts the **CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)** to recognize contributor roles:

- **Conceptualization**: Architecture & evaluation dimension design.
- **Software**: Core engine implementation, CLI automation, diagnostics.
- **Validation**: Test suites, quality gates, reproduction testing.
- **Documentation**: User guides, reports, citation specifications.

---

## ORCID Integration Policy

- Contributors are encouraged to add their **ORCID ID** (16-digit persistent digital identifier) to `CITATION.cff` and `.zenodo.json`.
- Example format: `0000-0002-1825-0097`.
