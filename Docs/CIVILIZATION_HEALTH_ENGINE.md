# Civilization Health Engine

The Civilization Health Engine measures the condition of the Tech Club civilization infrastructure through transparent, explainable metrics.

## Dimensions

- Knowledge Health
- Research Health
- Education Health
- Innovation Health
- Governance Health
- Community Health
- Trust
- Accessibility
- Open Science
- Collaboration
- Knowledge Preservation
- Global Participation

## Health Index

A Civilization Health Index includes metric explanations, evidence, Semantiq reports, uncertainty, question-origin coverage, federation optionality, offline readiness, human oversight coverage, technology independence, and an overall score.

Scores are decision support, not authority. A low score should generate questions, research, remediation plans, and review, not automatic punishment.

## Transparency

Every health report must disclose methods, evidence, assumptions, missing data, uncertainty, and who reviewed it.
