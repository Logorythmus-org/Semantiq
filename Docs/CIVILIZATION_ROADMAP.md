# Civilization Roadmap

The Civilization Roadmap turns evidence into adaptive long-term planning.

## Horizons

1 year, 5 years, 10 years, 25 years, 50 years, and 100 years.

## Inputs

Challenges, research progress, innovation pipeline, community participation, funding, technology trends, impact metrics, knowledge growth, and civilization health indicators.

## Rule

Roadmaps evolve from evidence, not centralized authority.
