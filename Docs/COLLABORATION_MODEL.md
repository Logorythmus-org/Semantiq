# Research Collaboration Model

Research collaboration supports individual and collective scientific work.

## Team Types
Individual Research, Open Community Research, Private Teams, Institutional Teams, University Projects, Citizen Science, AI-assisted Research, and Hybrid Human-AI Teams.

## Team Metadata
Roles, permissions, responsibilities, contribution history, trust, reputation, affiliations, and review constraints.

## Rule
Team collaboration uses identity, permissions, audit, and contribution tracking. AI agents assist but do not own scientific responsibility.
