# Collective Intelligence Engine

The Collective Intelligence Engine turns the Global Knowledge Mesh into a living distributed cognitive system. It is Human + AI + Knowledge + Communities working together, not AI replacing people.

## Core Flow
Question -> Communities -> Research Groups -> Evidence -> Semantiq -> Knowledge Graph -> AI Agents -> Innovation -> Public Benefit.

## Responsibilities
- Detect knowledge gaps and research opportunities.
- Coordinate research across federated communities and institutions.
- Support collective reasoning with evidence, uncertainty, debate, consensus, and minority reports.
- Maintain distributed collective memory.
- Forecast emerging questions, fields, collaborations, bottlenecks, and innovation opportunities.
- Measure global knowledge growth, discovery, education, and public benefit.

## Package Layout
- `packages/collective-intelligence/src/contracts.ts`: signals, gaps, opportunities, coordination, reasoning, memory, forecasts, analytics, maps, agents, repository, service, and event contracts.
- `packages/collective-intelligence/src/index.ts`: local service scaffold for gap discovery, forecasting, coordination, recommendations, global maps, analytics, growth measurement, and innovation forecasts.
