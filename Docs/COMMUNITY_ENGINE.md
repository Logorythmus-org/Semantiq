# Community Intelligence Engine

The Community Intelligence Engine transforms independent individuals into collective intelligence. It is not another social network, follower system, or reputation leaderboard.

## Core Flow

Questions -> Communities -> Collective Thinking -> Shared Knowledge -> Collaborative Research -> Innovation -> Public Benefit.

## Community Principle

Communities organize around questions, evidence, research, projects, knowledge, and innovation. The question, not the individual, is the center of gravity.

## Supported Communities

Communities, circles, research groups, learning groups, scientific teams, innovation labs, schools, universities, open communities, private communities, temporary communities, and hybrid human-AI communities.

## Package Layout

- `packages/community-engine/src/contracts.ts`: community, member, role, contribution, reputation, trust, consensus, analytics, agent, repository, service, and event contracts.
- `packages/community-engine/src/index.ts`: local repository/service scaffold.
- Future directories: `communities/`, `members/`, `roles/`, `permissions/`, `reputation/`, `trust/`, `contributions/`, `consensus/`, `analytics/`, `mentorship/`, `recommendation/`, `dashboard/`, `agents/`, `search/`, `knowledge/`, `api/`, `schemas/`, `events/`, `ui/`, `tests/`, and `docs/`.
