# Civilization Digital Twin

The Civilization Digital Twin is a semantic snapshot for analysis, exploration, simulation, and planning. It is not predictive certainty and must present uncertainty clearly.

## Scope
Snapshots can reference knowledge, research, education, innovation, communities, policies, infrastructure, technologies, challenges, and roadmaps.

## Use Cases
- Explore long-term effects of architecture evolution.
- Compare protocol migration paths.
- Identify gaps across education, innovation, governance, and research.
- Support sustainability planning.
- Explain roadmap choices through evidence.

## Boundaries
Digital twin outputs are advisory. They cannot approve policy, publish claims, move funds, change permissions, or replace human responsibility.
