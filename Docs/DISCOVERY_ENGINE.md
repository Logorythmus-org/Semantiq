# Discovery Engine

Discovery helps people find valuable questions.

## Discovery Modes
- newest
- trending by knowledge quality
- nearby topics
- scientific potential
- highest uncertainty
- recently updated
- most connected
- most researched
- most benchmarked
- unanswered
- needs evidence
- needs collaboration
- future AI recommendations

## Feed Architecture
The feed is not a popularity feed. Ranking uses question quality, scientific potential, learning value, collaboration need, evidence quality, semantic novelty, diversity, and curiosity.

## Anti-Patterns
The network must not optimize for likes, outrage, addictive scrolling, or engagement farming.
