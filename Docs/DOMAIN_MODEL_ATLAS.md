# Scientific Domain Model

The Atlas uses a flexible domain hierarchy.

## Built-In Domain Examples

Physics, Mathematics, Biology, Medicine, Chemistry, Astronomy, Earth Science, Engineering, Computer Science, AI, History, Philosophy, Psychology, Economics, Education, Arts, Language, Politics, Law, Culture, Environment, Energy, Space, and Future Technologies.

## User-Created Domains

Users may create domains and subdomains when existing taxonomy is insufficient. User-created domains carry provenance, moderation status, aliases, and graph relationships.

## Interdisciplinary Bridges

The Atlas detects bridges such as Physics to AI, Biology to Robotics, History to Linguistics, Mathematics to Economics, and Education to Neuroscience.
