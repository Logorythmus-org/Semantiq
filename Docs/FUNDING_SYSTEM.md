# Funding System

Funding supports knowledge creation and public benefit.

## Funding Types

Project funding, research funding, community funding, educational funding, innovation funding, open science grants, challenge bounties, question bounties, and public goods funding.

## Campaign Fields

Campaign identity, title, purpose, asset or project references, beneficiary, goal amount, raised amount, funding type, contributor records, milestones, audit history, and public-good status.

## Funding Without Paywalls

Public-good assets can remain free while accepting donations, sponsorships, grants, or matching funds.
