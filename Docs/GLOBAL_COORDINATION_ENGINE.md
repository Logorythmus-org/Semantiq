# Global Coordination Engine

The Global Coordination Engine coordinates communities, universities, research labs, governments, companies, NGOs, citizen scientists, developers, AI agents, and distributed nodes without central ownership.

Coordination starts from questions. A question can produce knowledge gaps, research plans, learning paths, challenges, governance processes, funding needs, agent goals, workflows, and roadmap updates.

## Runtime Model
- Participants keep autonomous identity, local policy, and local data authority.
- Coordination plans reference knowledge, research, education, innovation, governance, economy, agents, federation nodes, and oversight requirements.
- Semantiq evaluation is required before a coordination plan becomes operational.
- Human oversight is required for publication, policy, funding, identity, sensitive data, and high-impact decisions.

## Adapter Boundaries
The engine coordinates existing modules through adapters:
- Federation for cross-node exchange.
- Governance Engine for policy and deliberation.
- Innovation Network for challenges and prototypes.
- Education Network for learning and credentials.
- Semantic Economy for assets, funding, and public goods.
- Agent OS and Workflow Engine for execution.
- Compute Engine for distributed and local resources.

## Anti-Centralization Rules
No coordination plan may require a single global database, vendor, government, foundation, model provider, institution, wallet provider, or cloud platform.
