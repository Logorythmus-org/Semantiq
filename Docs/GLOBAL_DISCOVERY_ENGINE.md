# Global Discovery Engine

The Global Discovery Engine proactively identifies opportunities for new knowledge.

## Discovery Targets

Knowledge gaps, research opportunities, cross-disciplinary discoveries, emerging topics, unsolved problems, future technologies, societal challenges, educational needs, and scientific trends.

## Inputs

Questions, evidence, research activity, community activity, Semantiq benchmarks, federated search, distributed graph references, marketplace signals, public-good needs, and educational gaps.

## Principle

Discovery suggests opportunities. It does not centralize control over research agendas.
