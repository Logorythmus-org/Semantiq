# Global Marketplace

The marketplace becomes federated across nodes.

## Capabilities

Cross-node publishing, distributed licensing, federated search, distributed payments, asset mirroring, regional policies, local tax rules, and community governance.

## Rules

Marketplace exchange must preserve license terms, ownership, attribution, public-good status, trust records, and local policy.

## Public Goods

Federated public goods can be mirrored and funded across nodes without creating paywalls.
