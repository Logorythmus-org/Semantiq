# Global Research Map

The Global Research Map makes planetary knowledge work explorable.

## Visualized Entities

Questions, research projects, communities, universities, experts, datasets, repositories, experiments, innovation clusters, and funding opportunities.

## Interaction

Users can filter by topic, geography, institution, evidence state, Semantiq score, trust, community, challenge, dataset, and opportunity.

## Federation

The map references remote nodes without copying private data unless policy allows replication.
