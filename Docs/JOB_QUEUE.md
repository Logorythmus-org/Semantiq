# Job Queue

The Job Queue organizes compute tasks before execution.

## Queue Types

Immediate, background, scheduled, priority, retry, long-running, streaming, distributed, AI tasks, benchmark tasks, and graph tasks.

## Queue Behavior

Queues preserve task dependencies, retry policies, checkpoint references, permission requirements, resource requirements, and scheduling decisions.

## Backpressure

Large workloads require queue length monitoring, incremental scheduling, throttling, and graceful degradation.
