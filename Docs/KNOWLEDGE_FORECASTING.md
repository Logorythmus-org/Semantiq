# Knowledge Forecasting

Knowledge forecasting identifies likely future knowledge movement.

## Forecast Targets

Emerging questions, emerging fields, future collaborations, potential discoveries, educational trends, technology evolution, research bottlenecks, and innovation opportunities.

## Uncertainty

Forecasts include evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, confidence, alternative scenarios, and failure modes.

## Governance

Forecasts guide exploration. They do not determine truth or centrally direct research.
