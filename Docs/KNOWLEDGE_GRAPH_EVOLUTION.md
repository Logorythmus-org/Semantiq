# Knowledge Graph Evolution

The Knowledge Graph becomes temporal in Civilization OS.

## Temporal Graph Contents
Past states, present state, future predictions, lineage, historical snapshots, cross-generation links, knowledge migration, preservation metadata, and governance records.

## Historical Queries
Users can query what was known, disputed, trusted, preserved, migrated, or taught at a specific time.

## Preservation
Temporal graph records preserve provenance, source snapshots, migration paths, and persistent identifiers.
