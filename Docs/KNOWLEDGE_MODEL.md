# Atlas Knowledge Model

Every Atlas Entry represents a Question.

## Entry Fields

Identity, summary, context, domain, subdomain, difficulty, research status, importance, scientific potential, evidence, counter evidence, unknowns, assumptions, dependencies, hypotheses, experiments, publications, projects, datasets, repositories, experts, communities, benchmark history, knowledge evolution timeline, AI notes, and future directions.

## Knowledge States

Unknown, Observed, Explored, Evidence Growing, Actively Researched, Experiment Running, Verified, Disputed, Open Debate, Historical, Archived, and Reopened.

Multiple states may coexist when evidence is mixed or the question differs across domains.

## Scientific Domains

Physics, Mathematics, Biology, Medicine, Chemistry, Astronomy, Earth Science, Engineering, Computer Science, AI, History, Philosophy, Psychology, Economics, Education, Arts, Language, Politics, Law, Culture, Environment, Energy, Space, Future Technologies, and user-created domains.
