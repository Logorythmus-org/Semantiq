# Knowledge Quality

Knowledge quality is continuously evaluated through Semantiq and preservation analytics.

## Evaluation Dimensions
Knowledge growth, historical stability, evidence quality, scientific integrity, educational value, innovation, global accessibility, and long-term preservation.

## Preservation Quality
A high-quality preserved object has provenance, lineage, evidence, signed snapshots, readable formats, open standards, migration history, and accessible educational context.

## Governance
Quality scores are explainable and reviewable. They do not replace scientific debate, community governance, or human judgment.
