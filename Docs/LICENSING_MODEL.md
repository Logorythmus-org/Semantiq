# Licensing Model

Licenses are machine-readable contracts for reuse.

## Supported License Families
Open licenses, Creative Commons, commercial license, research license, educational license, community license, custom license, attribution required, share-alike, no-derivatives, internal use, and public domain.

## License Fields
License identity, asset identity, grantor, grantee, usage rights, attribution requirements, derivative policy, commercial policy, expiry, territory, compatibility notes, and audit reference.

## Enforcement
License enforcement is policy-based and auditable. Legal enforcement and payment-provider enforcement are future adapter responsibilities.

## Clarity
Every marketplace listing must expose license terms before claim, purchase, donation, sponsorship, or reuse.
