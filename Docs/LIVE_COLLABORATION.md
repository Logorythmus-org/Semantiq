# Live Collaboration

Live collaboration lets humans and AI agents work together in real time and offline.

## Capabilities

Real-time editing, presence, cursor sharing, selection sharing, comments, annotations, suggestions, review, conflict resolution, offline collaboration, merge, history, and time travel.

## Agent Collaboration

Agents can join workspace sessions for pair programming, research teams, writing teams, review teams, planning teams, presentation teams, education teams, and visualization teams through Agent OS.

## Conflict Resolution

Conflicts produce structured merge records with source versions, affected objects, resolution strategy, reviewer, audit trail, and graph impact.
