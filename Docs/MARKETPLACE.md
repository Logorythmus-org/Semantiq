# Marketplace

The marketplace helps people discover and reuse semantic assets without reducing quality to popularity.

## Marketplace Modes

Free assets, paid assets, open source assets, public goods, community assets, institutional assets, private sharing, subscription access, one-time purchase, donation, sponsorship, and funding campaigns.

## Search

Semantic search, asset type search, question-based search, project-based search, license search, creator search, community search, educational search, research search, and trust search.

## Ranking

Ranking should consider relevance, Semantiq quality, evidence, trust, license clarity, safety, educational value, research value, freshness, compatibility, and user intent.

## Publishing

Commercial publishing requires human approval, ownership verification, license validation, audit logging, and wallet protection.
