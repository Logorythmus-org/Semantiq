# Marketplace Agents

Marketplace agents assist users while remaining explainable and reviewable.

## Agent Roles
Asset Curator Agent, License Assistant, Pricing Assistant, Quality Reviewer, Trust Reviewer, Funding Agent, Recommendation Agent, Marketplace Moderator, and Attribution Agent.

## Boundaries
Agents may recommend, explain, review, flag risks, and draft metadata. They cannot silently publish commercial assets, change ownership, alter licenses, or execute payments.

## Explainability
Every recommendation includes reason, evidence, confidence, risks, and human-review requirements.
