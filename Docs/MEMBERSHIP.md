# Membership

Membership evolves over time.

## Member Fields

Identity, role, contribution history, expertise, interests, trust, permissions, learning progress, research activity, projects, achievements, benchmark history, and community reputation.

## Membership Modes

Open membership, invitation, application, institution-backed membership, temporary event membership, mentorship membership, and AI-agent membership.

## Rule

Membership is permissioned, auditable, and privacy-aware.
