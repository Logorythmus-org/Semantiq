# Open Science Infrastructure

Open science is native to the Innovation Network.

## Capabilities

Open research, open protocols, open data, open experiments, open peer review, open benchmarking, open education, open repositories, open simulations, and open innovation.

## Reproducibility

Research artifacts preserve datasets, protocols, environment references, evidence, reviews, benchmarks, lineage, licenses, and graph links.

## Access

Open science assets can be mirrored and funded without forcing paywalls.
