# Ownership Model

Ownership describes accountable control over a semantic object.

## Ownable Objects
Questions, projects, repositories, games, narratives, research, books, benchmarks, datasets, workflows, and agents.

## Ownership Modes
- single owner
- shared ownership
- organization ownership
- community ownership
- delegation
- transfer
- inheritance
- revocation

## Ownership Records
Ownership records include owner, asset, mode, share, permissions, provenance, effective dates, signatures, revocation state, and audit references.

## Transfers
Ownership transfers are explicit, auditable, and reversible only through policy-approved restore or revocation flows.
