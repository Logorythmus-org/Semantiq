# Ownership And Revenue

Ownership and revenue sharing remain transparent.

## Ownership Models

Single ownership, shared ownership, community ownership, organization ownership, royalty splits, contribution-based shares, donation routing, funding pools, and public benefit funds.

## Revenue Records

Revenue records include asset, transaction, recipients, split percentages, calculated amounts, currency, reason, audit reference, and wallet record references.

## Contribution-Based Shares

Contribution-based shares must reference contribution proofs, review records, Semantiq evidence, and human-approved allocation policy.

## Transparency

Users should see who owns an asset, who contributed, which license applies, and how revenue flows.
