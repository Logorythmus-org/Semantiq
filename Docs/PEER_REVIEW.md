# Peer Review

Peer review is transparent and configurable by research context.

## Review Modes
Open Review, Blind Review, Double Blind Review, Community Review, Expert Review, and AI-assisted Review.

## Evaluation Areas
Methodology, evidence, logic, reproducibility, transparency, ethics, novelty, and scientific potential.

## Rule
AI can assist review, but human reviewers make final decisions for publication or high-impact claims.
