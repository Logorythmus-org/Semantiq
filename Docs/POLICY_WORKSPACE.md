# Policy Workspace

Policies are living semantic knowledge objects.

## Policy Fields

Identity, intent, scope, evidence, research basis, affected communities, alternatives, version history, implementation status, impact, review schedule, and Knowledge Graph links.

## Evolution

Policies evolve through evidence, review, impact measurement, appeals, alternatives, and new questions.

## Traceability

Every policy links to the governance process, dialogue, consensus state, simulations, decisions, implementation records, and future review.
