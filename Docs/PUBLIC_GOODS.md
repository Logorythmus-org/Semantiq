# Public Goods

Public goods are reusable assets that remain accessible while still being fundable.

## Examples

Open research, educational games, question atlases, public datasets, open workflows, and community toolkits.

## Rules

Funding must not require paywalls. Public-good assets preserve free access, transparent funding, license clarity, and attribution.

## Sustainability

Funding routes can support maintainers, contributors, communities, public benefit funds, research groups, and educational programs.
