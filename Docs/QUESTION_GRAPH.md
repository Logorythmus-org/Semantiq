# Question Graph

Every question automatically enters the Knowledge Graph.

## Graph Objects

Question graphs visualize dependencies, evidence, projects, communities, researchers, repositories, experiments, games, narratives, and benchmarks.

## Relationship Types

Questions can relate through `extends`, `duplicates`, `contradicts`, `depends_on`, `supports`, `generalizes`, `specializes`, `derived_from`, `alternative_to`, `part_of`, `causes`, `explains`, and `future_work`.

## Relationship Metadata

Every relation includes confidence, evidence, creator, timestamp, version, and explanation.

## Updates

The graph updates automatically from question events, relationship events, discussion contributions, benchmark events, and workspace/project links.
