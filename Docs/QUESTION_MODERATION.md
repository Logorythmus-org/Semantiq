# Question Moderation

Moderation protects the quality and safety of the Question Network.

## Evaluation Areas
- scientific validity
- question clarity
- evidence quality
- respectfulness
- transparency
- spam
- duplicate detection
- safety
- legal compliance

## Human Judgment
AI can assist detection, summarization, duplicate search, and risk analysis. Humans make final moderation decisions for important cases.

## Outcomes
Moderation can request clarification, merge duplicates, flag risks, restrict visibility, archive, restore, or escalate.
