# Research Coordination

Research coordination helps global knowledge work self-organize.

## Participants

Researchers, communities, universities, companies, citizen scientists, AI agents, government labs, international projects, and open science initiatives.

## Coordination Objects

Research opportunities, coordination plans, roles, task groups, evidence needs, experiment needs, dataset needs, funding needs, review needs, and publication paths.

## Policy

Coordination respects node policy, regional compliance, research restrictions, human oversight, and local ownership.
