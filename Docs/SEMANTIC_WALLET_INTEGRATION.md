# Semantic Wallet Integration

The Semantic Wallet stores economy proofs and records without becoming the marketplace itself.

## Wallet Records

Owned assets, created assets, licensed assets, purchased assets, credentials, achievements, revenue records, contribution proofs, certificates, receipts, and digital signatures.

## Integration Points

Asset creation writes creator records. License grants write licensed-asset records. Purchases and claims write receipt records. Revenue distribution writes revenue records. Reviews and credentials can write achievement or proof records.

## Protection

Wallet operations require permission checks, audit records, digital signatures, and human approval for sensitive or commercial operations.
