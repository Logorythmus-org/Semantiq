# Semantiq Engine

Semantiq is Tech Club's semantic intelligence layer. It is not a traditional benchmark and not an AI leaderboard.

## Core Question
Semantiq asks: how well does this improve understanding?

## Benchmarkable Objects
Question, Answer, Discussion, Observation, Hypothesis, Evidence, Experiment, Project, Repository, Dataset, Research Paper, Conversation, Workflow, Agent, Narrative, Book, Video, Game, Presentation, and Community.

## Philosophy
Traditional benchmarks measure accuracy, speed, tokens, loss, parameters, and performance. Semantiq measures meaning, understanding, reasoning, evidence, creativity, scientific value, collaboration, reflection, uncertainty management, explainability, and knowledge growth.

## Rule
Benchmarking should guide deeper research and better learning. It must not become an opaque ranking machine.

## Package Layout
- `packages/semantiq/src/contracts.ts`: benchmark subjects, dimensions, scores, reports, profiles, comparison, history, recommendations, events, and engine contracts.
- `packages/semantiq/src/index.ts`: local explainable evaluator scaffold.
- Future directories: `engine/`, `pipeline/`, `reasoning/`, `evidence/`, `coherence/`, `creativity/`, `science/`, `ethics/`, `reports/`, `comparison/`, `history/`, `recommendation/`, `profiles/`, `weights/`, `schemas/`, `api/`, `events/`, and `ui/`.
