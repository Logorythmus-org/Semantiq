# Story Architecture

Stories are editable knowledge structures.

## Structures
Linear Story, Branching Story, Interactive Story, Investigation, Scientific Mystery, Historical Reconstruction, Engineering Challenge, Business Simulation, Ethical Dilemma, Open Exploration, Collaborative Story, and AI-generated Story.

## Story Objects
Narrative, Story Arc, Scene, Dialogue, Character, Role, Conflict, Choice, Challenge, Outcome, Reflection, Learning Goal, and Benchmark.
