# Trademark & Branding Policy

This document defines the branding guidelines and trademark usage policy for **SemantIQ Benchmarks**.

---

## 1. Permitted Uses

- You MAY refer to "SemantIQ Benchmarks" to truthfully describe your use of or compatibility with the software.
- You MAY distribute modified forks, provided you clearly disclose that the modified version is an unofficial fork.

---

## 2. Restricted Uses

- You MAY NOT use the name "SemantIQ" or the logo in a manner that implies official endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation without written permission.
