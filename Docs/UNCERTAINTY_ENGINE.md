# Uncertainty Engine

Uncertainty is a first-class citizen in the Atlas.

## Tracked Signals
Unknowns, assumptions, missing evidence, conflicting evidence, confidence, research gaps, open challenges, and future questions.

## Purpose
The engine helps users understand what is known, what is disputed, what is missing, and what should be explored next.

## Output
Uncertainty profiles expose explanation, confidence, evidence gaps, contradiction references, and suggested next investigations.
