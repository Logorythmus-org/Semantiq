# Workflow Templates

Workflow templates are editable starting points for repeatable goal execution.

## Template Categories

Research workflow, programming workflow, publication workflow, question refinement, benchmark workflow, education workflow, presentation workflow, translation workflow, repository review, community moderation, innovation sprint, and hackathon.

## Template Rules

Templates are versioned, benchmarked, explainable, editable, and tied to known inputs, outputs, approval checkpoints, risks, and reflection history.

## Marketplace Readiness

Published templates require validation, benchmark results, license metadata, author attribution, compatibility notes, and human approval.
