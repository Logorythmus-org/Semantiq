## Audit Evidence Bundle Rules

Audit evidence bundles MUST package `AuditScope`, `AuditCriterion`, `EvidenceInventory`, `MissingEvidenceRegister`, `AuditFinding`, and `AuditLimitation` into a reproducible offline format.
