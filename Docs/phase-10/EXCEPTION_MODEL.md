## 1. 12 Core Domain Objects

1. `ExceptionRequest`: Request details, requester ID, scope, and reason.
2. `ExceptionDecision`: Authority, decision state, duration, and conditions.
3. `ExceptionAuthority`: Authorized role and approval authority.
4. `ExceptionScope`: Scope component and allowed actions.
5. `ExceptionReason`: Reason code and justification text.
6. `ExceptionEvidence`: Verified supporting evidence reference.
7. `ExceptionDuration`: Time-bound duration window.
8. `ExceptionCondition`: Attached compliance conditions.
9. `OverrideRecord`: Emergency override log entry.
10. `OverrideImpact`: Severity level and residual risk assessment.
11. `ExceptionReview`: Post-incident review record.
12. `ExceptionClosure`: Exception resolution and closure log.
