# Governance Non-Goals Audit

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-02  

---

## Verified Non-Goals

1. **No Active Policy Enforcement**: SemantIQ records evidence without enforcing decisions.
2. **No Legal or Moral Certification**: SemantIQ does not issue legal compliance certificates or moral judgments.
3. **No Hidden Chain of Thought Access**: SemantIQ evaluates observable event logs only.
4. **No Unapproved Public Release**: SemantIQ remains strictly under local workspace Phase 7 publication freeze safeguards.
