# Non-Certification Boundary Specification

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-02

---

## Non-Certification Rules

- **SemantIQ Does Not Certify**: SemantIQ benchmarks evidence coverage; it DOES NOT issue legal certifications, compliance approvals, or regulatory guarantees.
- **Language Enforcement**: Claims asserting "certified" or "legal compliance guaranteed" trigger `false_certification_language` failure reports.
