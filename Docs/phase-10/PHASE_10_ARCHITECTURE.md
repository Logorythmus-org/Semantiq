# Phase 10 Governance Evidence Architecture

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-02

---

## Architectural Principles

1. **Provider-Neutral Evidence Layer**: SemantIQ records and evaluates evidence without active enforcement or regulatory authority.
2. **Immutable Raw Evidence**: Raw text and event checksums are kept separate from evaluator-derived interpretations.
3. **First-Class Uncertainty**: Missing evidence is never converted into compliance or safety approval.
4. **Reproducible Offline Replay**: Audit bundles are fully deterministic and testable offline.
