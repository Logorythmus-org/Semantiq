# Policy Lifecycle Specification

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-01  

---

## Policy Lifecycle States

1. `draft`: Initial policy proposal.
2. `proposed`: Under institutional review.
3. `active`: Active evaluation policy.
4. `deprecated`: Pending retirement.
5. `revoked`: Explicitly invalidated before expiration.
6. `expired`: Terminated by time window.
