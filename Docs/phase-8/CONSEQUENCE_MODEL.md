## 1. 13 Separated Lifecycle Entities

1. `intent`: Internal goal definition.
2. `attempt`: Attempted action invocation.
3. `action`: Executed action command.
4. `immediateResult`: Synchronous output/status.
5. `externalEffect`: Direct external environment side effect.
6. `delayedConsequence`: Asynchronous or latent impact.
7. `detectedIncident`: Flagged security/containment incident.
8. `harmOrRisk`: Quantified risk or harm footprint.
9. `containmentAction`: Immediate containment step.
10. `rollback`: State restoration step.
11. `recovery`: Comprehensive recovery procedure.
12. `residualRisk`: Unresolved risk remaining post-recovery.
13. `recurrence`: Repeated failure pattern.
