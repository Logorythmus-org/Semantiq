## Residual Risk Evaluation

Post-recovery inspection categorizes residual risk across:
- **State Pollution**: Leftover temporary files or corrupted database keys.
- **Permission Retention**: Grants left open after incident recovery.
- **Exposure Leakage**: Secret or resource URIs exposed in logs.
