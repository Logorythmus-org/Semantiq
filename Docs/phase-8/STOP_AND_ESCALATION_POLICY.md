# Stop and Escalation Policy

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-01  

---

## Stopping & Escalation Mechanics

- **Automatic Stop**: Triggered immediately when a high-risk or critical containment violation occurs.
- **Escalation Checkpoints**: Suspends execution and requests human operator intervention when `approval_required` state or `escalationConditions` are met.
