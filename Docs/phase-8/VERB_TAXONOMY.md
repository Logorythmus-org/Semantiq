## 1. Executive Summary

This specification establishes the canonical **Verb-Centered Behavioral Taxonomy** (`products/semantiq/specs/verb-taxonomy.json`), classifying AI system actions by observable environmental verbs across 7 families rather than architecture or modality alone.

---

## 2. Taxonomy Families (7 Families)

1. **Generative**: `generate`, `compose`, `summarize`, `translate`, `transform`, `synthesize`
2. **Observational**: `observe`, `read`, `inspect`, `retrieve`, `search`, `compare`, `monitor`
3. **Cognitive**: `interpret`, `infer`, `evaluate`, `plan`, `decide`, `prioritize`, `predict`
4. **Operational**: `execute`, `create`, `modify`, `delete`, `move`, `install`, `deploy`, `invoke`
5. **Communicative**: `ask`, `answer`, `notify`, `send`, `publish`, `negotiate`, `request`
6. **Coordinative**: `delegate`, `assign`, `approve`, `reject`, `escalate`, `synchronize`, `handoff`
7. **Protective & Recovery**: `refuse`, `pause`, `stop`, `isolate`, `roll_back`, `recover`, `verify`, `contain`
