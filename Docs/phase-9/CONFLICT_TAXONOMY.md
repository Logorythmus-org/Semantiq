## 1. 12 Conflict Domains

1. `goal`: Contradictory mission objectives.
2. `role`: Role overlap or boundary violation.
3. `authority`: Disputed authority delegation scope.
4. `permission`: Conflicting environment permission grants.
5. `resource`: Shared resource lock contention.
6. `timing`: Race condition or sequence timing conflict.
7. `context`: Divergent shared memory interpretation.
8. `evidence`: Contradictory evidence digests.
9. `decision`: Voting or consensus deadlock.
10. `execution`: Opposing tool execution actions.
11. `recovery`: Conflicting failure recovery actions.
12. `policy`: Contradictory safety policy rules.
