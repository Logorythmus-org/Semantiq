# Contradiction Detection Specification (8 Conflict States)

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-01

---

## 8 Conflict States

1. `explicit`: Direct verbal or vote contradiction.
2. `implicit`: Subtle behavioral or action divergence.
3. `resolved`: Successfully reconciled conflict.
4. `suppressed`: Dissent silenced without resolution.
5. `ignored`: Unaddressed conflict.
6. `recurring`: Repeated conflict pattern.
7. `cascading`: Multi-level downstream conflict.
8. `unresolved_without_accountable_resolver`: Conflict lacking assigned owner.
