## 1. 15 Canonical Interaction Types

1. `direct_message`: Point-to-point communication.
2. `broadcast`: One-to-many communication.
3. `request`: Action or resource query.
4. `response`: Reply to a request.
5. `clarification`: Ambiguity query or answer.
6. `proposal`: Action or decision candidate.
7. `counterproposal`: Alternative proposal.
8. `approval`: Explicit authorization.
9. `rejection`: Explicit prohibition.
10. `escalation`: Raising issue to human operator or lead.
11. `notification`: Status update.
12. `acknowledgment`: Delivery/receipt confirmation.
13. `timeout`: Exceeded wait window.
14. `cancellation`: Aborting pending request.
15. `handoff`: Transferring task context.
