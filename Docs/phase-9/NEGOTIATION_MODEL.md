## 1. 12 Negotiation Event Types

1. `proposal`: Initial policy or action candidate.
2. `counterproposal`: Alternative proposed option.
3. `amendment`: Modification to active proposal.
4. `support`: Explicit expression of agreement.
5. `objection`: Explicit expression of disagreement.
6. `withdrawal`: Retracting a proposal.
7. `vote`: Formal ballot record.
8. `abstention`: Declining to vote.
9. `veto`: Overriding veto action.
10. `compromise`: Negotiated middle-ground proposal.
11. `agreement`: Final consensus reached.
12. `deadlock`: Unresolvable disagreement state.
