## Phase 10 Boundary Constraints

Phase 10 evaluates **Governance Evidence**. Mandatory guidelines:
1. SemantIQ MUST NOT become a governance authority or policy enforcer.
2. SemantIQ MUST NOT weaken human checkpoint approval requirements.
3. SemantIQ MUST NOT publish public artifacts prior to Phase 12 completion.
