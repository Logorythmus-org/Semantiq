# Responsibility Transfer Specification

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Date**: 2026-08-01

---

## Responsibility Transfer Boundaries

- **Execution Transfer**: Delegated task step execution.
- **Authority Transfer**: Explicit authority scope granted to delegatee.
- **Outcome Responsibility**: Delegatee assumes direct accountability for execution outcome upon acceptance.
- **Accountable Actor**: Every delegation record MUST maintain an unassigned, explicit `accountableAgentId`.
