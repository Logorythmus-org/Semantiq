## Reading this matrix

`TESTED_COMPONENT` records source plus relevant tests, not a complete operational product. `SYNTHETIC_ONLY` records fixture behavior. `UNVALIDATED_PROXY` and `COMPUTED_NOT_VALIDATED` never imply a scientifically validated measure. Reported “confidence”, “signature”, “certification” and “verified” field names are not themselves evidence for those interpretations.

The focused next gate is S02_BENCHMARK_REGISTRY_AND_LIFECYCLE. Its evidence contract must connect definitions to actual observations and explicitly separate synthetic execution.
