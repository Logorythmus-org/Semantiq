# SemantIQ v0.1.0-alpha.1 Public Alpha Release Notes (Draft)

**Release Level**: Level 2 — Public Alpha (Experimental)  
**Date**: 2026-08-07  

---

## Canonical Release Statement

> SemantIQ Public Alpha is an experimental open-source evaluation and evidence infrastructure. Its results describe observed behavior under declared conditions. They are not certifications of intelligence, safety, legal compliance, or deployment suitability. The project is open to reproduction, criticism, dispute, correction, and forking.

---

## What Is Included

- Core benchmark execution and deterministic replay harness
- Machine-readable evidence manifests and schemas
- Append-only score dispute, correction, suspension, and withdrawal protocols
- Multilingual and cultural rubric assumption specifications
- Community governance and maintainer conflict-of-interest policies
- Project self-observation metrics and known blind spot registers
- 20-scenario adversarial red-team simulation suite

---

## Explicit Exclusions & Out-of-Scope Capabilities

- Authoritative global rankings or leaderboards
- Model safety or maturity certifications
- High-impact human decision automation
- Protected Tier D challenge prompts (isolated from public releases)
