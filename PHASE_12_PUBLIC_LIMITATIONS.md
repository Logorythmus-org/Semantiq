# Phase 12 Public Limitations Statement

**Project**: SemantIQ Benchmarks  
**Release Level**: Level 2 — Public Alpha (Experimental)  
**Date**: 2026-08-07

---

## Mandatory Public Limitations Notice

1. **External Replication & Independent Audit**: Independent third-party audit and external reproduction are not yet established; initial validation has been conducted via clean-room simulation and local test suites.
2. **Community Governance Scaling**: Community governance mechanisms (RFC proposals, council review, maintainer term reviews) have not yet been evaluated at public ecosystem scale.
3. **Benchmark Gaming Surface**: Public exposure and real-world benchmark optimization may uncover new failure modes or gaming strategies not observed in pre-release simulation.
4. **Configuration & Version Dependency**: All evaluation scores are strictly dependent on exact model version, timestamp, system prompt, temperature, and adapter configuration.
5. **Level 2 is not Level 3**: This release is an experimental research alpha, not a generalized public benchmark standard or certification authority.
