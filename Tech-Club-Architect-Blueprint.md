\# Tech Club

\#\# Project Vision

Tech Club is a Question-Centered Agentic Operating System designed for learning, research, invention, collaboration, education, digital ownership, and semantic computing.

Unlike traditional social networks that organize people around content consumption, Tech Club organizes people around meaningful questions, collaborative reasoning, and project creation.

Tech Club is independent from LogicNet.

LogicNet is a research project.

Tech Club is a product ecosystem.

The platform integrates AI agents, semantic benchmarking, local-first computing, WebGPU acceleration, secure data ownership, collaborative workspaces, scientific knowledge mapping, educational games, and decentralized semantic identity into one unified environment.

Semantiq Benchmark is the reasoning and semantic evaluation engine of Tech Club.

Unlike traditional AI benchmarks that primarily evaluate accuracy or language generation, Semantiq Benchmark evaluates the quality of questions, reasoning processes, explanations, collaboration, creativity, uncertainty management, and knowledge construction.

It provides a common evaluation framework for both humans and AI systems.

Semantiq Benchmark serves as the "measurement layer" of the entire Tech Club ecosystem.
