# Benchmark Exposure Tiers Specification

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-06  

---

## Benchmark Tiers Definition

1. **Tier A — Public Reference**: Fully open for education, reproduction, examples, and method inspection. Exportable in public bundles.
2. **Tier B — Rotating Evaluation**: Periodically replaced or regenerated on a declared schedule.
3. **Tier C — Transformational Evaluation**: Generated through controlled scenario variations while preserving construct validity.
4. **Tier D — Protected Challenge**: Restricted evaluation suites for leakage detection and release validation. NEVER exportable in public bundles.
