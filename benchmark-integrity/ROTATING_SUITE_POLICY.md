# Rotating Suite Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-06  

---

## Rotation Rules

1. Tier B benchmark suites undergo automatic rotation on declared schedules (e.g., 30 days).
2. Outdated prompt suites are deprecated and moved to Tier A for public archival.
3. Scores must declare the exact version of the rotating suite used during execution.
