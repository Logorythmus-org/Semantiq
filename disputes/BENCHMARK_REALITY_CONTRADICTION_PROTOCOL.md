**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-07

---

## Contradiction Management

When real-world deployment incidents or empirical evidence contradict a model's high benchmark score:

1. A `ContradictionReport` is submitted.
2. The benchmark engine reviews missing constructs or blind spots.
3. If valid, the score is annotated/suspended and a benchmark revision is triggered.
