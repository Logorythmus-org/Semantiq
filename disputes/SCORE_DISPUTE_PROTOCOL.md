# Score Dispute Protocol

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-07  

---

## Dispute Lifecycle States

1. `submitted`: Formal dispute submitted with evidence.
2. `acknowledged`: Received and logged in public dispute log.
3. `under_review`: Assigned to reviewers for investigation.
4. `evidence_requested`: Additional technical evidence requested from challenger or provider.
5. `partially_accepted`: Certain grounds accepted; others rejected.
6. `accepted`: Dispute accepted; correction or withdrawal initiated.
7. `rejected_with_reasons`: Dispute rejected with published written rationale.
8. `appealed`: Decision appealed to Rubric & Governance Council.
9. `resolved`: Final disposition reached.
10. `reopened`: New evidence surfaced reopening investigation.
11. `superseded`: Replaced by subsequent dispute resolution.
