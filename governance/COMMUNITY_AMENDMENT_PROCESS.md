# Community Amendment Process

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-07  

---

## 10 Amendment Stages

1. `public_proposal`: Submission of formal proposal.
2. `evidence_period`: Minimum 14-day public evidence gathering.
3. `structured_review`: Review Council analysis.
4. `conflict_disclosure`: Required maintainer conflict declarations.
5. `experimental_implementation`: Prototype feature testing.
6. `community_feedback`: Public RFC feedback period.
7. `recorded_decision`: Recorded decision in public log.
8. `appeal_window`: 30-day appeal window.
9. `versioned_adoption`: Formal adoption into core specs.
10. `scheduled_review`: Annual post-adoption review.
