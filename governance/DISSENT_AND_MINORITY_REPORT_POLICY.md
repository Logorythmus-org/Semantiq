# Dissent and Minority Report Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04  

---

## Protection of Dissent

1. **Append-Only Preservation**: Dissenting opinions attached to any decision record cannot be modified or deleted by majority vote.
2. **Public Visibility**: Minority reports are published alongside decision records in all official logs.
3. **Traceability**: Superseding decisions must reference and preserve prior dissenting viewpoints.
