# Emergency Governance Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04  

---

## Limits on Emergency Powers

1. **Strict Time Limits**: Emergency rules (e.g. publication freeze, rapid patch) must declare an explicit expiration date (maximum 30 days).
2. **No Permanent Emergency Rules**: Marking an emergency rule as permanent is strictly forbidden and rejected by automated validators.
3. **Mandatory Post-Mortem**: Every emergency intervention requires a public post-mortem and review within 14 days of expiration.
