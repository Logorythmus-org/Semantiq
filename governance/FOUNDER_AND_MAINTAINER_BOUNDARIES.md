# Founder and Maintainer Boundaries

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Roles and Limits

- **Founder Authorship**: Acknowledges initial project creation and historical contribution, but conveys zero permanent scientific or governance veto power.
- **Maintainer Responsibility**: Operational stewarding of code, infrastructure, and release guards. Maintainers are subject to governance review, recusal rules, and potential community replacement.
- **Scientific Authority**: Derived solely from empirical evidence, open peer review, and reproducible benchmark evaluations.
