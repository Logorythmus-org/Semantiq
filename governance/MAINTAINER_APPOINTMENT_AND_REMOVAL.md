# Maintainer Appointment and Removal Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-07  

---

## Maintainer Governance Rules

- Maintainers are appointed for explicit terms with scheduled review dates (`termReviewDate`).
- Inactive or non-compliant maintainers may be suspended or removed by multi-sig maintainer consensus.
- Temporary emergency suspensions expire automatically within 30 days unless ratified by community appeal.
