# Power Limitation and Distribution Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Overview

SemantIQ distributes governance authority across maintainers, contributors, and the broader research community to prevent concentration of power.

## Key Policies

1. **Recusal on Conflict**: Any maintainer with a financial, employment, or personal interest in an evaluation subject must recuse themselves from governance decisions regarding that subject.
2. **Multi-Signature Review**: Major policy or scoring changes require approval from multiple independent maintainers.
3. **Open Community Voting**: Major constitutional amendments require a structured public proposal and community feedback period.
