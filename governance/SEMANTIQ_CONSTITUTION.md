# SemantIQ Constitution

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Status**: Active  
**Date**: 2026-08-04  

---

## Preamble & Constitutional Foundation

SemantIQ evaluates observable behavior under declared conditions. It does not prove intelligence, safety, legal compliance, deployment suitability, or moral authority.

> Openness → Inspection → Reproduction → Criticism → Correction → Participation → Earned Trust

## Constitutional Principles & Invariants

1. **No Permanent Emergency Authority**: Emergency powers must expire automatically within a declared timeframe.
2. **No Unreviewable Governance Decision**: All decisions require evidence, appeal paths, and scheduled reviews.
3. **No Silent Deletion of Dissent**: Dissenting opinions are first-class append-only records.
4. **No Permanent Founder Veto**: Founders and maintainers do not hold permanent unchallengeable veto power or scientific authority.
5. **No Result Immune from Challenge**: Every published evaluation score can be disputed with evidence.
6. **No Rubric Immune from Amendment**: Rubrics remain open to community inspection, challenge, and improvement.
7. **No Mandatory Central Service Dependence**: SemantIQ is local-first and provider-neutral.
8. **No Governance Rule Without Version History**: Governance changes are versioned and traceable.
9. **No Claim That Open Source Alone Guarantees Trust**: Trust is earned through continuous empirical reproduction and openness to criticism.
10. **No Transfer of Human Responsibility to a Model or Score**: Final decision responsibility rests with identifiable human actors and institutions.
