# Sponsor Influence Disclosure Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-07  

---

## Sponsor Boundaries

Sponsors are strictly forbidden from receiving:
- Veto power over benchmark results or governance (`hasVetoPower: false`)
- Privileged prepublication or hidden benchmark access (`hasPrivilegedAccess: false`)
- Ranking or score guarantees (`hasRankingGuarantee: false`)
