# Appeal and Human Review Requirements

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Mandatory Human Review Controls

Where evaluation results inform high-impact outcomes:

- Notice of automated scoring influence must be provided to affected parties.
- An accountable human officer (`appealOwner`) must review contested decisions.
- Non-retaliation policies protect users exercising their right to appeal.
- Outcomes of appeals must be documented and retained for audit.
