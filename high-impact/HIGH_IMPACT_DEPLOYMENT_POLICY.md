# High-Impact Deployment Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Mandatory Requirements for High-Impact Deployment

Any deployment of an AI system evaluated by SemantIQ in a high-impact domain must establish:

1. An explicit `ResponsibilityChainRecord` identifying named human roles.
2. A documented non-automated alternative decision path.
3. An accessible, human-staffed appeal mechanism.
4. Scheduled periodic review and expiration dates.
