# Prohibited and Unsupported Uses Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Prohibited High-Impact Uses

SemantIQ benchmark scores must NOT be used as the sole or primary automated decider for:

1. Employment or termination decisions
2. Housing access or eviction
3. Educational admission, grading, or expulsion
4. Immigration, visa, or asylum determinations
5. Criminal suspicion, policing, or sentencing
6. Medical diagnosis, triage, or treatment selection
7. Credit, insurance, or welfare benefit eligibility
8. Legal guilt or civil liability
9. Voting, election, or political rights
10. Social credit or citizen ranking
