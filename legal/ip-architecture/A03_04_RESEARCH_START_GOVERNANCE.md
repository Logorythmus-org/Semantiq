## 5. Provenance model

## 6. AI-assistance model

## 7. Third-party input policy

## 8. Cyber sensitivity policy

CYBER_SAFE, CYBER_CONTROLLED, and CYBER_HIGH_SENSITIVITY route access and publication. Strategic Cyber defaults RESEARCH_PREPUBLICATION; high sensitivity never auto-promotes.

## 9. Contribution minimum

External material cannot silently enter restricted research. Identity/provenance, third-party declarations, and material AI-assistance disclosure are required. Research acceptance is not publication. Final DCO/CLA choice remains human/legal.

## 10. Licensing/open-core minimum

`CURRENT_PUBLIC_LICENSE_CHANGE = NOT_AUTHORIZED`; `RESEARCH_START_REQUIRES_RELICENSING = NO`. Research adjacency does not make work PUBLIC; PROTECTED stays outside the public repository; promotion requires rights/license review.

## 11. Publication state transition
