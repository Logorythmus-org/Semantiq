**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-06

---

## Multilingual Verification Process

1. **Translation**: Initial machine or human translation. Marked as `translated_unvalidated`.
2. **Review**: Human evaluation by qualified reviewers assessing conceptual and pragmatic fit.
3. **Validation**: Status updated to `validated_equivalent` only after human review and inter-rater agreement scoring.
