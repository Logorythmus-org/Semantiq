## Charter & Safeguards

The Rubric Review Council provides peer oversight for proposed rubrics.
- **Rotating Membership**: Terms are time-limited.
- **Conflict Disclosure**: Members must declare conflicts of interest.
- **Non-Final Authority**: Council decisions are subject to community appeal and constitutional boundaries.
