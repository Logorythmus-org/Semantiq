# Translation Is Not Validation

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-06

---

## Foundational Principle

> Translating a prompt or rubric into another language does NOT validate its construct equivalence in that language.

Equivalence requires empirical review across:

- Conceptual equivalence
- Pragmatic equivalence
- Cultural equivalence
- Difficulty equivalence
- Inter-rater agreement by native-speaking human reviewers
