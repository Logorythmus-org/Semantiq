## Documented Coverage Gaps

1. **Multimodal Evaluation**: Current benchmarks focus on text-based interaction.
2. **Real-Time Interactive Drift**: Evaluates static prompt scenarios rather than live streaming agents.
3. **Low-Resource Languages**: Multilingual validation requires further human reviewer coverage.
4. **Long-Horizon Stateful Memory**: Scenarios evaluate up to 16 conversation turns.
