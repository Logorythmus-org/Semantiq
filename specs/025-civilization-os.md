# Civilization OS Specification

## Purpose

Define Civilization OS: Tech Club's long-term open knowledge preservation layer for civilization memory, semantic archives, provenance, lineage, governance, standards, migration, digital heritage, and future-compatible knowledge evolution.

## Goals

- Ensure knowledge survives people, organizations, technologies, storage engines, AI models, databases, languages, hardware, networks, and standards.
- Preserve provenance and lineage for every knowledge object.
- Make historical knowledge evolution navigable through timelines, historical graphs, snapshots, benchmarks, communities, workflows, agents, and marketplace state.
- Support decentralized governance, open knowledge standards, digital heritage, persistent identifiers, and future migrations.
- Use Semantiq to evaluate evidence quality, scientific integrity, educational value, accessibility, and preservation health.

## Requirements

- Civilization memory links questions, knowledge, research, evidence, projects, innovation, education, historical memory, and civilization memory.
- Preservation supports long-term storage, immutable snapshots, semantic archives, historical versions, lineage, migration, open standards, redundant storage, and future recovery.
- Provenance stores creator, purpose, time, evidence, dependencies, sources, transformations, AI contributions, human contributions, reviews, and trust history.
- Governance supports community, scientific, educational, institutional, policy, ethics, transparency, appeals, review, and version governance without centralized ownership.
- Persistent identifiers remain stable for questions, projects, research, communities, repositories, datasets, assets, workflows, and agents.

## Architecture

Civilization OS composes Knowledge Graph, Federation, Collective Intelligence, Semantiq, Identity, Semantic Wallet, Storage, Workspace Runtime, Research Engine, Narrative Engine, Developer Platform, and System Integration. It owns memory, archives, lineage, timeline, provenance, governance, constitution, migration, preservation, standards, analytics, education, agent, API, and event contracts.

## Risks

- Archives can become unreadable without active format and schema migration.
- Governance can centralize unless amendment, appeals, and community governance remain explicit.
- Provenance can become incomplete if AI and transformation history are optional.
- Preservation can violate privacy unless archive scope, encryption, and policy are enforced.
- Persistent identifiers can drift without resolution and migration records.

## Testing

Future tests must cover preservation, historical queries, lineage, migration, integrity, persistent IDs, governance, archives, future compatibility, performance, offline archives, privacy, security, and regression behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Civilization OS architecture documentation exists.
- Memory, preservation, lineage, timeline, archives, standards, governance, constitution, digital heritage, migration, APIs, and decisions are documented.
- `@tech-club/civilization-os` exposes typed preservation and governance contracts.
- Every knowledge object can be represented with permanent provenance and lineage.
- Future migration and decentralized governance are explicit.

## Implementation Notes

This specification authorizes architecture documentation and contract scaffolding for Civilization OS. Production archival backends, legal governance workflows, identifier registries, migration engines, and public standards bodies require later implementation approval.
