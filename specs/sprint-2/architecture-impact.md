# Sprint 2 Architecture Impact

Sprint 2 extends the Sprint 1 runtime through `@tech-club/sprint2-runtime`. Existing Sprint 1 APIs remain unchanged. Semantiq, Question Intelligence, Graph Runtime, and Research Runtime are reused where possible.

New service and UI surfaces are descriptors that follow the repository convention established in Sprint 1. Storage remains memory-first with migration descriptors for future JSON, SQLite, PostgreSQL, and Neo4j adapters.
