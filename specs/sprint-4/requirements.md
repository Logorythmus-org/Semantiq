# Sprint 4 Requirements

- S4-ASSET: Every asset references the common Knowledge Object model and includes identity, provenance, license, permissions, documentation, compatibility, integrity, and audit history.
- S4-PACKAGE: Packages are versioned, machine-readable, human-readable, portable, verifiable, extensible, offline-installable, and storage-independent.
- S4-VALIDATION: Publication requires manifest, schema, dependency, compatibility, permission, license, docs, tests, security, Semantiq, human review, and approval.
- S4-INSTALL: Installation must show dependency and permission plans before mutating local state.
- S4-LICENSE: License metadata is machine-readable and not legal advice.
- S4-PLUGIN: Plugins never access kernel internals directly.
