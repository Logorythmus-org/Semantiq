# Alpha Scope

Spec ID: S6-SCOPE

## Included

Local identity, workspaces, questions, Question Intelligence, Semantiq, graph, research, workflows, limited agents, approvals, local marketplace, plugin sandbox, invitation-only federation, sharing, federated search, conflict resolution, export/import, docs and feedback.

## Disabled

Open federation, payments, crypto, NFTs, enterprise billing, autonomous publishing, public anonymous discovery and unrestricted remote agents.

## Validation

`publicAlphaScopeItems` and `freezeAlphaScope()`.
