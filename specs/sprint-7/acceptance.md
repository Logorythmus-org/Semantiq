# Acceptance

Spec ID: S7-ACCEPT

## Approval Gate Acceptance

The pre-implementation gate is satisfied when:

- The repository reality audit exists and clearly distinguishes verified code, scaffolds, stubs, adapter-ready components, broken commands and unsupported claims.
- Sprint 7 requirements exist and are specific enough to implement.
- Consent and privacy design explains data collection, non-collection, retention, withdrawal, export and deletion.
- Initial experiment registry exists with stable IDs, thresholds and privacy implications.
- Risks and blockers are documented.

## Sprint Completion Acceptance

Sprint 7 itself is accepted only when:

- The real repository state has been audited.
- A controlled tester cohort exists.
- Invitation and revocation work in the selected alpha surface.
- Consent is granular and enforced.
- Core functionality remains available without telemetry.
- Onboarding has been tested with real users.
- The first-question experience has been validated.
- Product metrics exclude private content by default.
- Usability sessions have been completed.
- Semantiq feedback has been collected.
- AI suggestion quality has been evaluated.
- Human-control mechanisms have been validated.
- Reliability targets have been measured.
- Alpha issues have been triaged.
- At least one evidence-based UX improvement cycle has completed.
- Documentation has been validated by testers.
- Update and rollback have been tested with a recovery point.
- Public Alpha findings are documented.
- A Beta Readiness Report exists.
- No unresolved blocker remains hidden.
- Known limitations are public and accurate.
