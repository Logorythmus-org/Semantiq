# Deployment Suitability Boundary Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04  

---

## Deployment Boundary Rules

1. **No Automatic Deployment Approval**: A high score on a SemantIQ benchmark does not authorize real-world deployment.
2. **Context Independence**: Benchmarks evaluate isolated test scenarios, not complex operational environments with real-world feedback loops.
3. **Human Responsibility**: Final deployment decisions remain the sole responsibility of deploying organizations and accountable human operators.
