# No Certification Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Canonical Policy Statement

> SemantIQ evaluates observable behavior under declared conditions. It does not certify any system as safe, reliable, legally compliant, intelligent, or suitable for a specific deployment.

## Prohibited Claims

- "SemantIQ Certified"
- "Certified Safe Model"
- "Legally Compliant AI"
- "Production Ready Certification"

Any public usage of SemantIQ names or scores to claim official certification is unauthorized and misleading.
