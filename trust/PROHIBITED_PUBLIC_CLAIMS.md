# Prohibited Public Claims Policy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Forbidden Language

The following claims are strictly prohibited from all public outputs, summaries, and marketing:

- Claims of human-like understanding ("model thinks", "model understands")
- Claims of absolute safety ("certified safe", "100% safe")
- Claims of universal superiority ("best in the world", "smartest AI")
- Claims of legal or regulatory compliance ("legally compliant AI")
- Unscoped rankings lacking execution metadata
