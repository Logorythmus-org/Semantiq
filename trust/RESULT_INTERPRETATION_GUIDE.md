## Guidelines for Reading SemantIQ Benchmark Results

1. **Check the Scope Block**: Verify model version, execution timestamp, and temperature settings.
2. **Review Confidence Bounds**: Consider measurement variance rather than point scores.
3. **Inspect Raw Evidence**: Examine individual prompt responses and evaluator logs.
4. **Recognize Known Exclusions**: Note modalities or languages not tested in the run.
5. **Read the Mandatory Disclaimer**: Remember that benchmark results do not equal real-world safety or readiness.
