# Right to Fork and Reproduce

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04  

---

## Practical Forkability & Evidence Portability

1. **Evidence Portability**: All evaluation runs export standardized, local-first raw evidence datasets.
2. **Independent Reproduction**: Anyone can clone the repository, run benchmarks locally, and compare outputs without depending on centralized infrastructure.
3. **Right to Fork**: The codebase, schemas, and specifications are MIT licensed, guaranteeing the right to fork governance, rubrics, and engine implementations.
