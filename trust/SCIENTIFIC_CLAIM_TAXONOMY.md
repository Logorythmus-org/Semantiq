# Scientific Claim Taxonomy

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Date**: 2026-08-04

---

## Recognized Claim Classes

1. **observation**: Directly recorded output or event during evaluation execution.
2. **measurement**: Quantitative metric calculated from observable data.
3. **evaluator_judgment**: Structured rating assigned by an automated or human evaluator.
4. **aggregate_finding**: Summary statistic across multiple evaluation runs or scenarios.
5. **inference**: Analytical conclusion derived from aggregate findings (requires supporting evidence).
6. **recommendation**: Suggested follow-up or research direction based on findings.
7. **unsupported_claim**: Claim lacking empirical evidence or scope metadata (PROHIBITED from publication).
8. **prohibited_claim**: Claim claiming safety certification, legal compliance, or universal superiority (PROHIBITED from publication).
